Description

ZERO CONTACT: Redacted is a 2D arcade stealth puzzle designed specifically for mobile devices. Players step into the shoes of Agent Green, a shadow tasked with infiltrating ultra-secure complexes to recover stolen secret documents.

Unlike most contemporary mobile titles, ZERO CONTACT follows a "Premium First" model: a clean experience with no in-app purchases and no intrusive advertisements, ensuring total immersion in the gameplay.

Key Features

  • Pure Stealth: No combat system. Your only weapons are invisibility and planning.
  • 70 Handcrafted Levels: Each level is a unique environmental puzzle with guard patterns and cameras to study.
  • Strategic Progression: 10 unlockable abilities (EMP, Ghost Mode, etc.) to adapt your infiltration style.
  • Dual Structure: A free 17-level "Prologue" (starring Blanco) and the full "The Secret Documents" game.
  • Refined Pixel Art: Native support for Light and Dark modes, with an aesthetic inspired by retro classics.
  • Localization: Fully translated into 7 languages (IT, EN, ES, FR, DE, JA, SQ).
